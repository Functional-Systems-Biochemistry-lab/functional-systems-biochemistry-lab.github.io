## Background

Biological functions emerge from many molecules acting together rather than from single genes or proteins. Systems biology studies these interactions as networks and asks how a network behaves under perturbation, such as disease or drug treatment. Multi-omics data from mass spectrometry provide the measurements, and computational analysis turns them into testable models.

## Approach

Quantitative data from several omics layers are combined and analyzed with statistical and network methods, such as pathway enrichment and correlation networks. Hypotheses that come out of the analysis are tested back in the laboratory.
