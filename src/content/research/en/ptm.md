## Background

After a protein is made, it can be chemically modified at specific amino acids. These post-translational modifications, such as phosphorylation, glycosylation, acetylation, and ubiquitination, switch protein activity, change localization, and control interactions. Many diseases involve altered modification patterns. Modified forms are often present in small amounts, so they are hard to detect without enrichment and sensitive instruments.

## Approach

Proteins are digested into peptides, and modified peptides are enriched with affinity or chemical methods. LC-MS/MS then identifies the modification site from the fragmentation spectrum and quantifies how its occupancy changes between conditions.
