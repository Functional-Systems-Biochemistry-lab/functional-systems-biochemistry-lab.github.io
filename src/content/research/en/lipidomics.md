## Background

Lipids are more than energy stores and membrane building blocks. They act as signaling molecules, and their composition changes in many diseases, including metabolic, neurological, and inflammatory conditions. Lipidomics aims to identify and quantify the many lipid species in a sample, which differ in class, chain length, and degree of saturation. Mass spectrometry is the main technique for this work because it can resolve these closely related molecules.

## Approach

Lipids are extracted from the sample and separated by liquid chromatography before measurement by high-resolution or triple quadrupole mass spectrometry. Species are identified from accurate mass and fragmentation patterns, and quantified against internal standards so that samples can be compared.
