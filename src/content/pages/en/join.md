## What you will do and learn

- Prepare biological samples for proteomics, lipidomics, or imaging experiments
- Run LC-MS/MS and MALDI-TOF instruments and keep them in good condition
- Quantify proteins, lipids, and modifications, and apply the right statistics
- Interpret results in their biological and clinical context
- Build and document analysis pipelines, mostly in R or Python
