Kwang Pyo Kim is a Professor in the Department of Applied Chemistry at Kyung Hee University, where he leads research in mass spectrometry–based proteomics, lipidomics, and integrated multi-omics. His work centers on the discovery of disease-specific biomarkers through quantitative analysis, and extends to MALDI-TOF imaging mass spectrometry, the analysis of post-translational modifications and protein nitration, and systems biology. By applying these analytical technologies to clinical samples, his group aims to identify markers that enable the diagnosis of disease.
