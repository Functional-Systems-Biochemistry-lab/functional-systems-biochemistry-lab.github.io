## Directions


From Kyung Hee University Main Gate (Neo-Renaissance Gate): Take Bus 5100, get off at the stop inside the campus, and walk uphill for about 6 minutes toward the Kyung Hee Bus Depot and Baseball Field. (Approx. 11 minutes total / Approx. 20-30 minutes on foot directly from the Main Gate)

(Note: Other bus routes entering the campus—such as 9, 1112, 1560B, 7000 and M5107-can also be used to reach the same stop.)


From Yeongtong Station: Take Bus M5107, get off at the Sasaek Square (Sasaegui Gwangjang) stop, and walk uphill for about 6 minutes toward the Kyung Hee Bus Depot and Baseball Field.

(Note: Other bus routes entering the campus—such as 1112,5100,7000—can also be used to reach the same stop.)
