We study how biological systems work, from molecules to whole organisms, using mass spectrometry–based multi-omics: proteomics, glycomics, lipidomics, and imaging. We apply these methods to disease diagnosis, biomarker discovery, and drug development. If you are interested in our research, please get in touch or visit the lab.
